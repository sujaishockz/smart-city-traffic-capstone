# Traffic Visualization Interpretations

Figures are generated from the cleaned and engineered dataset. Weather averages are descriptive and can be affected by differences in time-of-day and day-type coverage.

- Average weekday traffic peaks around 16:00 (6189.5 vehicles per hour), while weekend traffic peaks around 12:00 (4372.5 vehicles per hour). The curves show how demand timing differs by day type.

- Clouds has the highest observed average traffic (3618.0) and Squall the lowest (2061.8), a difference of 1556.2 vehicles per hour. These means are based on 15158 Clouds and 4 Squall observations, respectively; the rare-category estimate should be interpreted cautiously.

- Temperature and traffic have a Pearson correlation of 0.132 in the cleaned observations. The scatter shows substantial variation at similar temperatures; this is association, not evidence that temperature causes traffic changes.

- Traffic volume ranges from 0 to 7280 vehicles per hour; the median is 3379. The distribution includes both quiet overnight periods and busy commute periods.
